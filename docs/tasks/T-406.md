# T-406 — Proof review deadline, late flag, and reject-and-block

## Goal
A buyer's manual payment proof never waits silently. During a live drop the seller is warned after 60 minutes, otherwise after 6 hours. A seller can reject a fake proof and keep that buyer out of the shop in one tap.

## Context
- PO decision 2026-11-11, recorded in `docs/prd.md` §6 and `docs/app-flow.md` §7.
- Stock paths 5 and 6 in `docs/database-schema.md` §6a; ADR 0005 (release + `PromoteFit` in one transaction).
- Copy: `docs/design.md` §9 "Seller: proof review".
- Errors: `buyer-blocked` in `docs/backend.md` §4.

## Scope
- In:
  - migration with `orders.review_due_at`, `orders.review_late_at`, and table `shop_buyer_blocks` (with the tenant RLS policy);
  - `reviewer` worker;
  - late flag + dashboard banner + `wa.seller_review_late`;
  - buyer cancel of `proof_submitted` after 6 h;
  - "Tolak dan blokir pembeli di toko ini"; blocked-buyer list with unblock;
  - block checks in claim and `PromoteFit`.
- Out: auto-reject (explicitly not wanted), cross-shop blocks (PRD non-goal).

## Interfaces
```sql
ALTER TABLE orders ADD COLUMN review_due_at timestamptz, ADD COLUMN review_late_at timestamptz;
CREATE INDEX orders_review_due_idx ON orders (review_due_at)
  WHERE status = 'proof_submitted' AND review_late_at IS NULL;
CREATE TABLE shop_buyer_blocks (
  seller_id  uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES sellers(id),
  buyer_id   uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES buyers(id),
  reason     text NOT NULL CHECK (char_length(reason) BETWEEN 3 AND 500),
  order_id   uuid REFERENCES orders(id),
  created_at timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),
  PRIMARY KEY (seller_id, buyer_id));
-- + ENABLE/FORCE RLS and the tenant policy, as for every tenant table
```
- `order.SubmitProof`: sets `review_due_at = $now + 60 min` if the drop is `live` at that moment, otherwise `$now + 6 h`.
- `order.FlagLateReviews(ctx, now)`, called by `reviewer` every 30 s under `system`:
  - sets `review_late_at` on due rows (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`);
  - enqueues one `wa.seller_review_late` per seller per drop per hour (dedupe key `seller:<id>:drop:<id>:review_late:<hour>`).
- `order.Cancel` accepts `proof_submitted` when `$now >= proof_submitted_at + 6 h` (stock path 6).
- `order.Reject(ctx, sellerID, orderID, reason, blockBuyer bool)`, one transaction under `system`. With `blockBuyer`, it also:
  - inserts into `shop_buyer_blocks`;
  - sets the buyer's `waiting` entries in this seller's drops to `skipped`;
  - writes `audit_log` `buyer.block`.
- `trust.IsBlocked(tx, sellerID, buyerID)` is used by `order.Claim` (→ `buyer-blocked`) and `waitlist.PromoteFit` (→ `skipped`).
- Routes: `POST /seller/orders/{id}/reject` `{reason, block_buyer}`, `GET /seller/blocks`, `DELETE /seller/blocks/{buyerID}` (audit `buyer.unblock`).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Fake clock: a proof submitted during a live drop is flagged at +60 min (not at +59). A proof submitted after the drop closed is flagged at +6 h. No order is ever auto-rejected.
2. The seller gets at most one WA warning per drop per hour, plus a dashboard banner with the count.
3. A buyer cannot cancel before +6 h (`invalid-transition`). After that, cancel releases stock and promotes the waitlist in the same transaction; `invariant.CheckStock` passes.
4. Reject-and-block is atomic: forcing a failure after the block insert leaves the order `proof_submitted` and no block row.
5. A blocked buyer gets `buyer-blocked` on claim in that shop and can still claim in another shop. Their queued entries in that shop are skipped, and the next buyer is promoted.
6. Unblock lets the buyer claim again; both actions are in `audit_log`.

## Test expectations
- Unit: deadline computation (live vs not live at submit).
- Integration: worker with fake clock; reject-and-block atomicity; RLS on `shop_buyer_blocks` (seller B cannot see seller A's blocks).
- Concurrency: reject-and-block racing a claim by the same buyer and a promotion of that buyer.

## Risks and notes
- Sellers can misuse blocks; that is acceptable because they are per shop only and can be lifted.
- The block reason is visible only to the seller and the owner, never to the buyer.
